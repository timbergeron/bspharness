# Third-party tools and local assets

The repository does not vendor game data, texture libraries, skyboxes, or
compiler binaries. Their local download/extraction directories are ignored.

- [ericw-tools](https://github.com/ericwa/ericw-tools) provides qbsp, vis,
  light, and inspection utilities. Pinned release URLs and SHA-256 hashes
  are in `configs/tools-lock.json`; bundles retain their license files.
  Consult upstream's GPL and bundled Embree/TBB notices when redistributing
  tools. The harness does not distribute those binaries in Git.
- Original Quake PAK content belongs to id Software and its applicable
  licensors. The `id-wad` command uses locally supplied PAKs for map
  development. Do not commit the source PAKs, generated WAD, or palette.
- Optional WAD sources and pack-specific provenance guidance are in
  `docs/TEXTURES.md`. Credit the actual materials embedded in each map.
- [QSS-M](https://github.com/timbergeron/QSS-M) is the target QA engine; use
  a local build/install. The engine and id1 data are not included.

Procedural development textures generated by `bspharness/wad.py` are original
patterns. They do not contain copied palette bytes or third-party art.
Makkon assets are neither included nor fetched by the harness.
