Awoken - Quake 1 adaptation

Layout reference: Awoken from Quake Champions.
Recovered Quake 2 remake brushwork: 4Bidden, credited in the original
worldspawn as "Awoken --> By 4Bidden".
Reference mirror: http://q2.packetflinger.com/dl/baseq2/maps/awoken.bsp

Quake 1 conversion, material mapping, lighting and movement checks:
bspharness (https://github.com/timbergeron/bspharness).
Textures and stock gameplay: original Quake, id Software; extracted locally
from the owner's pak0.pak/pak1.pak. The native texture pass imports the 26
user-supplied PNGs recorded in source/recipe/art.json. All native
masters retain original dimensions and bytes. Runtime surface PNGs upscale
to powers of two to avoid QSS-M odd-width mipmap row skew; neither axis is
reduced. The skybox is
projected from the supplied panorama; waterfall frames are lossless row
scrolls. Indexed BSP textures are portable fallbacks. Original PNGs and
local WAD accompany the editable source. No Makkon assets are used.
Compilation: ericw-tools, pinned 2.0.0-alpha11.

Xowoken r1 by AriosJentu was inspected as a secondary visual/source
reference. No Xowoken geometry, models, or textures are imported.
Release page: https://forums.xonotic.org/showthread.php?tid=10192

INSTALL
Extract maps/, textures/, and gfx/ into the SAME Quake game directory.
Keep awoken.bsp and awoken.lit together in its maps/ folder. The textures/
and gfx/ folders supply the high-resolution appearance. In QSS-M set
"gl_load24bit 1", "gl_max_size 0", "gl_picmip 0", "r_fastturb 0", and
"r_fastsky 0" before loading the map. Do not put the PNGs in maps/.
BSP/LIT alone remain playable with small indexed fallback textures.
No custom QuakeC or external texture WAD is required to play. A normal
local Quake installation and a modern engine with masked textures and
high-resolution external PNG support are recommended.

For deathmatch, enter "deathmatch 1" then "map awoken" in the console, or
select awoken when hosting a game. For an exploration pass use
"deathmatch 0" then "map awoken". This is a deathmatch arena with eleven
DM starts, not a single-player campaign level; there are no monsters or
campaign exit. Quad is excluded from every SP skill and retained in DM.

ADAPTATION
This is a stock Q1 reinterpretation of the Q2 remake. The railgun becomes
lightning, machinegun becomes nailgun, and chaingun/hyperblaster become
super nailguns. Slugs become large cell packs; bullets and former cells
supply nails. Other weapons, health/mega, armor and quad use stock id1
entities. Three pads angle toward their landing ledges to accommodate Q1
air acceleration; the fourth already has a directed launch. Teleport and
void-hurt brushes are retained. Rotating pad ornaments become nonblocking
static details. The video polish restores 45 vine brushes as masked,
nonblocking details; trees, bushes, grass, alpha decals and mist stay omitted.
Eleven recessed relief assemblies have modeled stone surrounds and no
collision. The geometry pass eases exposed stone corners and shapes
seventeen upper cornices; their player collision is rebuilt from those
visible profiles. The inherited waterfall uses the supplied art in eight-frame
animation and nonblocking translucent brush models. Geometry notes and the exact recipe accompany the source. Neutral stone,
green water, daylight and cool landmark lighting follow the user's supplied
Awoken video reference. The original stock release is retained separately.

Build, material seams, runtime entity checks, authored movement probes,
actual DM spawn/item audit and reviewed camera evidence accompany this
local package. They establish the sampled routes and artifact identity;
a populated match is still the next test for timing, tactics and balance.
This is not an official Quake Champions release or an exact visual port.
Original map and texture assets remain the property of their creators.
