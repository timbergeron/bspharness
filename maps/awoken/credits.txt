Awoken - Quake 1 adaptation

Layout reference: Awoken from Quake Champions.
Recovered Quake 2 remake brushwork: 4Bidden, credited in the original
worldspawn as "Awoken --> By 4Bidden".
Reference mirror: http://q2.packetflinger.com/dl/baseq2/maps/awoken.bsp

Quake 1 conversion, stock material mapping, lighting and movement checks:
bspharness (https://github.com/timbergeron/bspharness).
Textures and stock gameplay: original Quake, id Software; extracted locally
from the owner's pak0.pak/pak1.pak. No Makkon assets are included.
Compilation: ericw-tools, pinned 2.0.0-alpha11.

Xowoken r1 by AriosJentu was inspected as a secondary visual/source
reference. No Xowoken geometry, models, or textures are imported.
Release page: https://forums.xonotic.org/showthread.php?tid=10192

INSTALL
Copy maps/awoken.bsp and maps/awoken.lit into your Quake game directory's
maps/ folder. Keep the two files together. A modern Quake engine such as
QSS-M is recommended. No custom QuakeC or external texture WAD is needed
to play this compiled BSP. A normal local Quake installation is required.

For deathmatch, enter "deathmatch 1" then "map awoken" in the console, or
select awoken when hosting a game. For an exploration pass use
"deathmatch 0" then "map awoken". This is a deathmatch arena with eleven
DM starts, not a single-player campaign level; there are no monsters or
campaign exit. Quad is excluded from every SP skill and retained in DM.

ADAPTATION
This is a stock Q1 reinterpretation of the Q2 remake. The railgun becomes
lightning, machinegun becomes nailgun, and chaingun/hyperblaster become
super nailguns. Slugs become large cell packs; bullets and former cells
supply nails. Other weapons, health/mega, armor and quad use stock id1
entities. Three pads angle toward their landing ledges to accommodate Q1
air acceleration; the fourth already has a directed launch. Teleport and
void-hurt brushes are retained. Rotating pad ornaments become nonblocking
static details. Q2 alpha vegetation/decals/mist are omitted.

Build, material seams, runtime entity checks, authored movement probes,
actual DM spawn/item audit and reviewed camera evidence accompany this
local package. They establish the sampled routes and artifact identity;
a populated match is still the next test for timing, tactics and balance.
This is not an official Quake Champions release or an exact visual port.
Original map and texture assets remain the property of their creators.
