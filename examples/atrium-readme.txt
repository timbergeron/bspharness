BSP Harness: Atrium

An original generated Quake 1 layout demonstrating a central sky-lit atrium,
two side rooms, an elevated armor bridge, and a lower circulation loop.
This is a harness demonstration/blockout, not a playtested map release.

Geometry and procedural blockout textures: bspharness / Tim Bergeron.
Stock material variant: textures extracted locally from id Software's
Quake pak0.pak and pak1.pak. No source PAKs or WADs are distributed here.
Compiler: ericw-tools by Eric Wasylishen, Jon Gold, and contributors.
Engine verification: QSS-M and contributors.
No Makkon textures or skyboxes are used.

See build.json for source/compiler hashes, format, and exact build commands.
See validation.json for BSP checks. QA reports, when included, cover runtime
spawn/item checks and screenshot collection; playtesting remains necessary.
